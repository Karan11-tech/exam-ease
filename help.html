Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the most common questions.

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password," enter your email, complete the OTP verification, then set your new password.

How can I download study materials?

Log in to the Student Dashboard, open the Study Materials section, and use Preview or Download on any material. PDFs are generated when you download.

How do I contact admin for doubts?

Use the "Doubts & Discussion" section on the Student Dashboard to post your question. Admins reply from the Admin Dashboard; you'll see answers in the same section and get a notification.
